How pricing works

Three steps before you commit to anything.

01

Discovery call

30 minutes, no pitch deck. I listen to your challenge and current stack, then tell you honestly what I'd recommend and roughly what tier it falls into.

02

Fixed proposal

I review your setup and send a clear scope, timeline and fixed fee within 48 hours. You know the cost before any work begins.

03

You decide

No pressure, no expiring discount countdown. If the scope changes later, we agree the change and the price before it happens, not after.
